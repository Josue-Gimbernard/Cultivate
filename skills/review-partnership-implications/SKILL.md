# Review Partnership Implications

Use this skill for Foxtail or any future Cultivate partner concept.

## Steps

1. Identify whether the material is internal planning, partner-facing, or approved commitment.
2. Read relevant partner file under `domains/partnerships/`.
3. Identify mutual value, partner requirements, obligations, risks, and open questions.
4. Check downstream implications for Brand, Operations, Growth, Spaces, and Product.
5. Avoid promising exclusivity, volume, financial terms, employment terms, or co-branding without explicit approval.
6. Record approved durable terms in the partner file.
