# Extract Open Questions

Use this skill when source docs, planning sessions, or domain reviews reveal unresolved decisions.

## Steps

1. Identify the source of the question.
2. Classify the question:
   - naming
   - launch
   - compliance
   - facility
   - staffing
   - business model
   - partnership
   - brand/public promise
3. Add durable questions to `memory/10-open-questions.md`.
4. Add temporary or unclear questions to `memory/inbox.md`.
5. Remove or update questions when the founder makes a decision.
