# Make Operating Decision

Use this skill for company-level operating decisions, governance choices, sequencing, or source-of-truth questions.

## Steps

1. Identify the decision being made.
2. Identify current facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Check relevant memory and domain files.
4. Separate reversible decisions from commitments.
5. Record approved durable decisions in `memory/06-recurring-decisions.md`.
6. Add unresolved follow-ups to `memory/inbox.md`.

## Safety

Do not turn a proposal into a commitment without explicit founder approval.
