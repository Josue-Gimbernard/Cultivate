What The Commons Is

A flexible space that serves the broader community

The Commons is Cultivate’s multi-use space — a flexible, welcoming room available for homeschool co-ops, community organizations, small events, workshops, and private gatherings. It’s designed to be a resource not just for Cultivate families, but for the broader homeschool and local community.

When it’s not in use by Cultivate programs, The Commons is available to rent — making it both a community asset and a sustainable revenue stream that helps keep Cultivate membership costs accessible.

What It’s Used For

Versatile by design

Homeschool Co-Ops & Classes

The Commons is an ideal home for homeschool co-ops looking for a reliable, well-equipped meeting space. Whether you’re running a weekly class, a study group, or a full co-op day, The Commons offers the room, the amenities, and the community infrastructure to make it work.

Workshops & Class Rentals

Educators, specialists, therapists, and community experts can rent The Commons to run their own workshops and classes. From art instruction to financial literacy seminars to speech therapy groups, The Commons is a professional, welcoming space for community learning of all kinds.

Community Meetings & Events

Local organizations, parent groups, and nonprofits can use The Commons for meetings, information sessions, and community gatherings. It’s a space that belongs to the community — and we want the community to use it.

Private Parties & Celebrations

Birthdays, graduations, milestone celebrations — The Commons can be rented for private events that want a warm, unique setting outside of a traditional event venue. Catering coordination through the Harvest Café is available for events booked through Cultivate.

Booking & Pricing

Simple, transparent, community-friendly

Cultivate members receive discounted rental rates for The Commons. Non-member community organizations and individuals are welcome to book at standard rates. Co-ops and nonprofits may qualify for reduced community rates — reach out to discuss.

Booking Options

Half-day blocks (morning or afternoon)

Full-day rental

Recurring weekly or monthly bookings for co-ops and regular groups

After-hours evening and weekend availability for private events

All bookings are subject to availability and Cultivate’s community use guidelines. Priority scheduling is given to active Cultivate members and homeschool community organizations.

Why It Matters

Shared spaces build stronger communities

The homeschool community in Central Florida is large, active, and underserved when it comes to quality shared spaces. The Commons exists to fill that gap — giving co-ops, educators, and families a professional, welcoming place to gather, learn, and celebrate together. Every booking supports the Cultivate ecosystem and helps keep teen and family programming accessible.
