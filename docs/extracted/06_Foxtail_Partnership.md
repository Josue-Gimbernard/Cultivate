A Partnership Concept

Foxtail Coffee × Cultivate

Bringing great coffee to a community that’s doing something meaningful

Confidential concept for discussion purposes • Cultivate • Central Florida

Who We Are

Cultivate — a homeschool family campus in Central Florida

Cultivate is a new kind of community space for homeschool families in Central Florida. Built around a teen life skills and enrichment program, Cultivate is designed to serve the whole family under one roof: teens in The Glade, young children in The Sprout Space childcare center, and parents in The Grove co-working lounge.

At the center of it all is The Harvest Café — a community gathering spot that connects every part of the campus. It’s where people linger, meet, and feel at home. And it’s where we believe Foxtail belongs.

The Opportunity

A captive, loyal, values-aligned audience

Foxtail has built something rare in Florida: a local coffee brand with genuine community credibility and a loyal following that actively chooses it over national chains. Cultivate’s audience is exactly the kind of community Foxtail resonates with — thoughtful, independent, locally-rooted families who care about quality and authenticity.

The Numbers

Daily on-site presence of homeschool families, teens, and remote-working parents

Multi-hour dwell times — parents working in The Grove, teens in The Glade all day

Events and co-op rentals in The Commons bringing additional community traffic

A membership model that builds habitual, recurring visits

A demographic that is highly word-of-mouth driven — the homeschool community talks

What We’re Proposing

A branded outpost with a community twist

We are interested in exploring a licensed Foxtail outpost or branded partnership within The Harvest Café — bringing Foxtail’s coffee quality and brand to our campus while preserving the community-first, teen-centered spirit of the space.

What This Could Look Like

A Foxtail-branded coffee bar within The Harvest Café serving Foxtail’s core menu

Teens from The Glade trained and employed as baristas through Foxtail’s program

Foxtail branding integrated alongside Cultivate’s Harvest Café identity

Complementary food offerings (pastries, snacks) provided by Cultivate teens

Co-branded community presence at campus events and The Commons programming

The Teen Employment Angle

One of the most compelling aspects of this partnership is the potential for a teen employment pipeline. Teens at Cultivate are actively building life and vocational skills. Foxtail barista training — with its emphasis on craft, customer service, and professionalism — aligns perfectly with what we’re building. Teens who train through Foxtail at Cultivate leave with a real credential and a genuine employment pathway.

This is a story worth telling. It’s exactly the kind of community impact both brands could be proud of.

What We’re Asking For

A conversation, not a commitment

This document is an invitation to explore, not a formal proposal. We would love to sit down with the Foxtail team to discuss whether there’s a partnership structure that works for both sides — operationally, financially, and culturally.

We believe great coffee and a great community make each other better. We’d love to find out if you agree.

Questions We’d Love to Explore Together

Does Foxtail have a licensed outpost or partnership program?

What are minimum volume or operational requirements for a partnership location?

Is there appetite for a teen employment or training component?

What does a co-branding arrangement typically look like for Foxtail?

We’d love to start a conversation.
