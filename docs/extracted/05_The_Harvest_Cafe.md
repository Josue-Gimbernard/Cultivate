What The Harvest Café Is

A real café run by real teens

The Harvest Café is Cultivate’s community café — a welcoming gathering spot serving coffee, tea, fresh-made pastries, healthy snacks, and smoothies. What makes it unique is who’s behind the counter: teens from The Glade who are learning the real business of food service as part of their life skills development.

This isn’t a simulation or a school project. It’s a functioning café where teens learn to plan a menu, manage food costs, serve customers, operate a point-of-sale system, and take pride in something they built with their own hands.

What’s Served

Simple, quality, community-made

Freshly brewed coffee and specialty drinks

Herbal teas and other non-coffee options

Fresh smoothies and light blended drinks

Member-made pastries, baked goods, and light bites

Healthy snacks — included in Glade membership, available for purchase to all

The menu is intentionally simple and rotates seasonally — guided by what teens are learning to make, what’s in season, and what the community loves.

The Teen Business Experience

Entrepreneurship education that pays for itself

Working in The Harvest Café is one of the most hands-on life skills experiences Cultivate offers. Teens who participate in café operations learn:

Menu planning and recipe development

Food costing and basic profit/loss thinking

Customer service and professional communication

Food safety and kitchen hygiene (with certification)

Point-of-sale operation and basic financial transactions

Marketing and community promotion

Teens who work in The Harvest Café leave with a real employment reference, transferable food service skills, and firsthand experience running a small business operation — skills most young adults don’t develop until years later.

Who It Serves

The whole Cultivate community and beyond

The Harvest Café is open to everyone on the Cultivate campus — teens in The Glade, parents in The Grove, visitors to The Commons, and families dropping off little ones at The Sprout Space. It’s the connective tissue of the campus: the place where people from every part of Cultivate cross paths, linger, and feel at home.

The Harvest Café is also available to cater events booked in The Commons, providing a seamless, community-made food experience for workshops, celebrations, and community gatherings.

A Note on Partnership

Open to collaboration

Cultivate is open to exploring a partnership with an established local coffee brand to bring professional-quality coffee to The Harvest Café — while preserving the teen-run, community-first spirit of the space. Any partnership would be structured to keep teens at the center of the café experience, including employment, training, and operations.

If you are a local coffee brand interested in being part of something meaningful, we’d love to talk.
