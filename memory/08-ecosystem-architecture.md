# Ecosystem Architecture

## Current Anchor

Cultivate is a homeschool family campus in Central Florida for ages 0-18. The source promise is "Real Skills for Real Life."

Current canonical ecosystem source: `docs/extracted/09_Cultivate_Full_Ecosystem_FINAL.md`.

Provenance: the final ecosystem doc and The Wilds doc came from the owner as updated concept material. Treat them as current higher-authority sources unless the owner or founder clarifies otherwise.

## Current Components

Indoor campus:

- The Sprout Space: ages 0-5, on-site childcare.
- The Stepping Stones: ages 6-12, middle kids community space.
- The Summit: ages 13-18, teen community and life-skills space.
- The Harvest Cafe: teen-run community cafe.
- The Grove: parent co-working lounge.
- The Commons: multi-use community and rental space.

Outdoor campus:

- The Wilds: outdoor campus.
- The Sprout Yard: outdoor space for ages 0-5.
- The Range: shared all-ages movement/play space.
- The Canopy: teen outdoor retreat.
- The Growing Grounds: working garden.
- The Garden Guild: term-based group tending The Growing Grounds.

Community systems:

- 5-week term rhythm.
- Family contribution discounts.
- Community Fund.
- Village Wall.
- Founding family cohort.

## Version Notes

Earlier sources used `The Glade` for the teen community space. The final ecosystem source uses `The Summit`. Treat The Summit as current unless the founder says otherwise.

Earlier sources described mandatory family volunteer days. The final ecosystem source describes family contribution as optional and discount-based. Treat optional contribution as current unless the founder says otherwise.
