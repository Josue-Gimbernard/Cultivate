# Operating Model

## Starter Model

Cultivate starts with a company-agent architecture organized around the source-derived ecosystem:

- Company: strategy, governance, source-of-truth, and cross-domain coordination.
- Product: product/service definition and roadmap detail.
- Brand: voice, identity, positioning, and public language.
- Growth: customer acquisition, partnerships, sales, and market learning.
- Operations: internal systems, finance-sensitive process, admin, and execution cadence.
- Research: market, customer, domain, and reference research.
- Spaces: indoor/outdoor campus components and their dependencies.
- Partnerships: partner concepts, partner-facing boundaries, and external relationship strategy.

## Operating Rhythms From Source

- Cultivate uses a 5-week term concept.
- Weeks 1-4 carry focused programming.
- Week 5 is open, reflective, and useful for open houses and new member enrollment.
- Family contribution is currently framed as optional and discount-based.
- The Community Fund is optional and donation-supported.
- The Village Wall celebrates donors, volunteers, Garden Guild members, projects, photos, and community moments.

## Review Rhythm

Until a formal cadence exists, end meaningful sessions by updating relevant markdown and placing unresolved items in `memory/inbox.md`.
