# Company Safety Rules

- Do not invent launch dates, prices, commitments, legal positions, financial obligations, public promises, sponsor obligations, or investor-facing claims.
- Keep current facts, hypotheses, proposals, and decisions separate.
- Do not change live systems, accounts, websites, databases, public materials, or payment systems without explicit approval.
- Do not treat rough brainstorming as approved strategy.
- If a statement would create external expectations, ask before making it durable as a commitment.
- Treat Foxtail partnership material as discussion-only until explicitly approved.
- Treat founding family, launch, pricing, membership, staffing, compliance, and facility details as unapproved until explicitly decided.
