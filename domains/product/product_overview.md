# Product Overview

## Role

Product owns the product or service definition, user value, roadmap detail, and delivery scope.

## Current State

Cultivate's current product is a homeschool family campus ecosystem. The core offers are age-based programming, parent workspace, cafe/community gathering, shared space rentals, outdoor campus experiences, garden learning, and contribution-supported community membership.

## Current Product Areas

- The Sprout Space: childcare for ages 0-5.
- The Stepping Stones: middle kids programming for ages 6-12.
- The Summit: teen life-skills/community programming for ages 13-18.
- The Grove: parent co-working.
- The Commons: rentable community space.
- The Harvest Cafe: teen-run cafe and catering.
- The Wilds: outdoor play, teen retreat, and garden campus.
