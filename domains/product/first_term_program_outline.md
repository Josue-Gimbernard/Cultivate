# First Term Program Outline

## Purpose

This is a draft structure for Cultivate's first 5-week founding cohort term. It is internal planning, not public copy.

## Term Theme

Build the Village.

The first cohort helps build the culture, rhythms, and first visible artifacts of Cultivate.

## Weekly Shape

### Week 1: Belonging And Baseline

Goals:

- establish safety and culture
- learn family needs
- introduce the Cultivate ecosystem
- start skills inventory

Possible activities:

- family orientation
- teen circle and project interests
- middle-kid making project
- parent workspace/community setup
- Village Wall first entries

### Week 2: Food, Care, And Community

Goals:

- test communal rhythm without operating as a cafe
- explore food, hospitality, and care as life skills

Possible activities:

- simple meal-planning workshop
- snack/lunch budgeting exercise
- hospitality/customer-service roleplay
- parent-led skill share
- discussion of future Harvest Cafe vision

### Week 3: Making And Repair

Goals:

- test hands-on practical skills
- see how teens and middle kids respond to real projects

Possible activities:

- basic repair/fix-it workshop
- tool safety orientation
- build a portable Village Wall piece or Wilds sign prototype
- creative media documentation

### Week 4: Outdoors And Stewardship

Goals:

- test Wilds/Garden Guild concepts in low-risk form
- explore responsibility and consistency

Possible activities:

- container garden or raised-bed pilot
- nature observation
- garden schedule simulation
- older-younger mentorship moment
- discussion of The Wilds vision

### Week 5: Open And Reflect

Goals:

- gather feedback
- observe interest from prospective families
- decide what changes next term

Possible activities:

- project showcase
- parent feedback circle
- teen feedback circle
- middle-kid show-and-tell
- prospective family visit window
- next-term interest collection

## Roles To Test

- teen project lead
- middle-kid maker
- parent workshop contributor
- community host
- documentation/media helper
- garden steward

Do not frame any role as paid employment unless employment rules have been validated.

## Outputs By End Of Term

- family feedback summary
- next-term interest list
- revised launch scope
- staffing estimate
- facility must-have list
- pricing model inputs
- open compliance questions
