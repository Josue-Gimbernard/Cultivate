# Founder Decision Memo 001: First Launch Shape

## Decision

Choose the first public operating shape for Cultivate.

## Recommendation

Use a limited founding cohort as the first launch shape.

## Why

The full Cultivate ecosystem is compelling, but it includes regulated and facility-heavy components: childcare, cafe/food service, paid teen roles, public rentals, outdoor play equipment, insurance, zoning, and accessibility.

A limited founding cohort lets Cultivate validate demand, culture, programming, parent engagement, and the 5-week rhythm before making expensive or regulated commitments.

## Recommended First Offer

Internal name: Founding Cohort Term.

Duration: one 5-week term.

Audience:

- homeschool families in Central Florida
- teens ages 13-18
- middle kids ages 6-12
- parents who want workspace/community

Not included at first unless validated:

- childcare for ages 0-5
- public cafe
- Foxtail partnership
- paid teen employment
- public Commons rentals
- full outdoor playground/campus

## Decision Options

### Option A: Community Pilot

Smallest and least risky. Could be free or low-cost discovery sessions.

Best if: the founder wants learning before selling.

Tradeoff: less revenue proof.

### Option B: Limited Founding Cohort

Small paid or application-based first term with clear boundaries.

Best if: the founder wants to test willingness to commit while staying careful.

Tradeoff: requires more prelaunch paperwork and operational clarity.

### Option C: Full Campus Launch

Try to launch the whole concept.

Best if: facility, funding, licensing, staffing, and insurance are already close.

Tradeoff: highest complexity and risk.

## Current Recommendation

Option B: Limited Founding Cohort.
