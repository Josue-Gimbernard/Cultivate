# Brooke Approved Direction

## Positioning

Cultivate is a community space for homeschool families where everyone belongs.

At its heart, Cultivate helps teens build real-world skills and self-awareness to grow into capable, purposeful adults within the warmth of a whole-family community. It is not one thing. It is a living, working community where kids grow, families connect, and everyone has a place.

Public-facing framing should describe Cultivate as a community space for homeschool families where everyone belongs, not a school, tutoring center, STEAM program, or daycare.

The first page should feel like:

- home away from home
- excited to come
- relieved to be close
- independent but together

## Founder Story

The founder story may say that Brooke is a homeschool parent of two teens who spent years searching for a community that truly fit her family. She listened to her own kids and their friends, heard what they actually needed, and built it.

Her background includes years of hands-on homeschool parenting and operational experience directing a STEAM enrichment program.

Brooke's previous employer or program should not be named publicly. It may be referenced only as prior experience in enrichment program leadership.

## Day-One Offerings

- The Summit: teen community space, ages 13-18, and the core of Cultivate.
- The Grove: parent co-working lounge with self-serve coffee station and comfortable seating.
- The Village Wall: founding community display including build-out photos, staff, ribbon cutting, and founding families.
- The Stepping Stones: ages 6-12, confirmed if family demand and qualified staff support it.
- Simple outdoor space: shaded seating, hammock chairs, and grassy area, described as the beginning of something bigger.

## Membership Model

- 5-week term billing cycle.
- 1-, 2-, or 3-day per week pass options to start.
- 3-day pass structured as the best per-day value.
- Volunteer contribution discount:
  - weekly contribution: 20% off
  - monthly contribution: 10% off
  - occasional contribution: 5% off that month
  - no contribution: standard rate, no penalty
- Optional Community Fund for financial and supply donations.
- Step Up for Students accepted as soon as provider status is confirmed.

Specific pricing stays private until location and operational costs are confirmed.

## CTA And Interest List

The call to action is interest list only: warm, simple, and no commitment.

The form should collect:

- name
- email
- zip code
- ages of children
- how they heard about Cultivate
- one open field for what matters most to their family

Placeholder proof language while proof is gathered:

> Cultivate is currently gathering input from homeschool families across Central Florida. Early response has been overwhelmingly positive. Founding families are forming now - join the interest list to help shape what Cultivate becomes.

## Pricing Copy

Approved pricing direction:

Cultivate uses a flexible term-based membership model. Families choose how many days per week work for them. Founding family pricing will be announced once a location is confirmed. Cultivate plans to accept Step Up for Students scholarship funds. Families who contribute their time to the community receive a membership discount.

Do not publish specific prices until location and operational costs are confirmed.

## Still Private

- Childcare, The Sprout Space, and anything related to DCF licensing.
- The Harvest Cafe, any food or beverage sales, or cafe licensing.
- The Foxtail partnership or any named coffee or food brand partnership.
- Paid teen employment or apprenticeships.
- The Commons and space rentals.
- The Garden Guild until outdoor space is confirmed.
- The full Wilds outdoor campus beyond a simple aspirational mention.
- Brooke's previous employer or program.
- Specific pricing until location and operational costs are confirmed.
- Any family quotes, photos of minors, or questionnaire data without signed consent.

## Needs Validation

- Physical space: location, zoning, and permitting.
- Whether outdoor space is available at the chosen location.
- Qualified staff for The Stepping Stones before confirming it as day-one.
- Step Up for Students provider application and approval.
- Family and teen demand, validated through the three questionnaires.
- Photo release and testimonial consent forms before any family content is published.
- Legal review of participant waivers and liability documentation.
- Insurance: general liability appropriate for a youth program in Florida.
- Background check policy for volunteers, TBD in consultation with legal and insurance advisors.
- LLC formation or nonprofit determination.
- Pricing structure, dependent on space costs and operational overhead.

## Open Questions For Later

- LLC vs. nonprofit: which structure best serves Cultivate's mission and funding needs?
- Step Up for Students: what does the approved provider application process require and how early can it begin?
- Background check policy for volunteers: what does Florida law require and what does liability insurance recommend?
- Staffing model: who are the first hires or contractors beyond Brooke?
- The Stepping Stones: is there a qualified facilitator in Brooke's network already?
- Pricing: once a space is identified, what is the break-even membership number and what does that mean for per-term pricing?
- Foxtail: when is the right time to have that conversation and who is the right contact?
- Garden Guild and Growing Grounds: what does the chosen space allow for outdoors?
- Nonprofit grants: if Cultivate moves toward a nonprofit structure, what local and state grants are available for youth programming and homeschool community spaces in Central Florida?
- The questionnaires: when do they go out and through what channels does Brooke reach the homeschool families she is targeting?
