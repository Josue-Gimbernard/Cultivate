# Naming And Message Map

## Current Brand Anchor

Cultivate: Real Skills for Real Life.

## Approved Public Positioning

Cultivate is a community space for homeschool families where everyone belongs.

It should not be framed publicly as a school, tutoring center, STEAM program, or daycare.

The first page should feel like home away from home: exciting to come to, relieving to have close by, and independent but together.

## Current Owner-Approved Naming And Direction

- The Summit: ages 13-18; core teen community space.
- The Grove: parent co-working lounge with community coffee station.
- The Village Wall: founding community display.
- The Stepping Stones: ages 6-12; conditional on family demand and qualified staff.
- Simple outdoor space: aspirational day-one element if the chosen location supports it.

## Future-Facing Or Private Naming

- The Sprout Space: ages 0-5; private until childcare/DCF path is validated.
- The Harvest Cafe: future-facing only.
- The Commons: future-facing only.
- The Wilds: future vision only beyond simple outdoor-space language.
- The Sprout Yard.
- The Range.
- The Canopy.
- The Growing Grounds.
- The Garden Guild: private until outdoor space is confirmed.
- The Community Fund.

## The Glade Versus The Summit

`The Glade` carries warmth, softness, and a sheltered place to grow.

`The Summit` carries progression, arrival, aspiration, and readiness. It fits the final journey language: Sprout Space to Stepping Stones to Summit to World Ready.

Decision: use The Summit as current public naming, while preserving The Glade as earlier-source provenance and possible language inspiration.

## Public Message Boundaries

Safe to say:

- Cultivate is a community space for homeschool families where everyone belongs.
- The Summit is a teen community space for ages 13-18 and the core of Cultivate.
- The Grove is a parent co-working lounge with self-serve coffee and comfortable seating.
- The Village Wall will celebrate the founding community.
- The Stepping Stones is conditional on family demand and qualified staff.
- A simple outdoor space is part of the vision if the chosen location allows it.
- Cultivate is currently gathering input from homeschool families across Central Florida.
- The model centers real-life skills, belonging, inclusion, and community.
- Founding families are forming through an interest list.

Do not say yet:

- opening date
- prices
- licensed childcare is available
- Foxtail is a partner
- cafe is operating
- exact facility capacity
- paid teen work or apprenticeships are available
- Commons rentals are available
- family quotes, minor photos, or questionnaire data without signed consent
