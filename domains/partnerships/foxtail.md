# Foxtail Coffee Partnership

## Status

Concept for discussion only. Not a commitment.

## Proposed Fit

Foxtail could bring local coffee credibility and professional coffee quality to The Harvest Cafe while Cultivate provides a values-aligned audience of homeschool families, teens, remote-working parents, Commons visitors, and recurring members.

## Possible Structure

- Licensed Foxtail outpost or branded partnership inside The Harvest Cafe.
- Foxtail core coffee menu.
- Teen barista training/employment pathway.
- Co-branding with Harvest Cafe identity.
- Cultivate-provided pastries/snacks.
- Co-branded presence at events and Commons programming.

## Questions For Foxtail

- Does Foxtail have a licensed outpost or partnership program?
- What are the minimum volume or operational requirements?
- Is teen employment/training possible?
- What co-branding structures are available?
