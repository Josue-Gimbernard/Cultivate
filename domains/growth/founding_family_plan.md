# Founding Family Plan

## Purpose

Recruit early families around participation, feedback, and culture-building before making fixed launch promises.

## Positioning

Cultivate is a community space for homeschool families where everyone belongs. At its heart, it helps teens build real-world skills and self-awareness within the warmth of a whole-family community.

## Founding Family Promise

Founding families help shape Cultivate through a warm, no-commitment interest list and follow-up questionnaires.

Avoid promising:

- exact opening date
- final pricing
- childcare availability
- cafe operation
- Foxtail partnership
- facility location
- all spaces at launch
- paid teen work or apprenticeships
- Commons rentals
- family quotes, photos of minors, or questionnaire data without signed consent

## Interest List Fields

- name
- email
- zip code
- ages of children
- how they heard about Cultivate
- what matters most to their family

## Discovery Questions

- Which child age groups would your family use?
- What days/times would actually work?
- What is the biggest pain in your current homeschool rhythm?
- Would parent co-working matter?
- Would you use childcare for ages 0-5?
- Would you rent or attend events in The Commons?
- What would make The Summit feel safe and valuable for your teen?
- What would you contribute if contribution is optional?
- What price range feels possible?

## Early Channels

- Local homeschool groups.
- Co-ops.
- Parent Facebook groups.
- Neurodiverse homeschool communities.
- Local therapists/specialists.
- Coffee/community partners.
- Existing founder network.
