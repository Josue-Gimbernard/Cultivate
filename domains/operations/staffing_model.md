# Staffing Model

## Status

Internal planning model only. No staffing commitments exist.

## Staffing Principle

Cultivate depends on warm, skilled adults and carefully bounded teen responsibility. Teen work is a learning pathway, not a replacement for licensed or accountable adult staff.

## Launch Staffing Areas

### Company/Operations Lead

- Enrollment and family communication.
- Term calendar.
- Facility coordination.
- Vendor/regulator contact.
- Incident and recordkeeping workflow.
- Volunteer/contribution coordination.

### Summit Facilitators

- Teen community leadership.
- Life-skills workshops.
- Project supervision.
- Inclusion support.
- Communal meal rhythm.

### Stepping Stones Facilitators

- Middle-childhood programming.
- Hands-on projects.
- Group rhythm and conflict support.
- Bridge programming with The Summit.

### Sprout Space Staff

Only after childcare licensing path is confirmed.

- Qualified lead educators.
- Required credentials/background checks/training.
- Direct supervision of any teen helpers.
- Licensing records and parent communication.

### Grove Host

- Parent workspace support.
- Desk/day-pass management.
- Community connection.
- Basic office amenities.

### Harvest Cafe Lead

Only after food-service path is confirmed.

- Cafe operations.
- Food safety.
- Teen worker training.
- POS/cash handling.
- Menu and inventory.

### Commons Coordinator

- Bookings.
- Setup/cleanup.
- Rental agreements.
- Insurance/waiver requirements.
- Community use guidelines.

### Wilds/Garden Lead

- Outdoor safety.
- Garden Guild schedule.
- Tool/supply storage.
- Planting and harvest rhythm.
- Cross-age supervision.

## Teen Roles To Validate

- Cafe trainee/barista.
- Sprout Space assistant.
- Stepping Stones junior mentor.
- Garden Guild member.
- Workshop/project assistant.
- Commons event helper.

## Safety Rule

Every teen role needs a written boundary: allowed tasks, prohibited tasks, required training, supervision level, age requirements, records, and emergency procedure.
