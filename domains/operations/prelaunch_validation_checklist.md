# Prelaunch Validation Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist defines what must be validated before Cultivate publicly offers or sells a founding cohort.

## Minimum Validation Before Public Interest Collection

- Confirm owner/founder approval for public-facing concept language.
- Confirm whether the first offer is called pilot, founding cohort, beta term, preview term, or another name.
- Confirm what age groups are included.
- Confirm that childcare is not promised unless licensing is validated.
- Confirm that cafe/food service is not promised unless food-service path is validated.
- Confirm that teen roles are learning/volunteer roles unless employment path is validated.
- Confirm that no Foxtail partnership language implies commitment.
- Confirm contact method and data collection process.

## Minimum Validation Before Taking Money

- Entity/payment path.
- Refund policy.
- Liability waiver reviewed.
- Insurance path.
- Facility/site permission.
- Supervision plan.
- Emergency contact and medical info process.
- Background screening policy for adults working with minors.
- Parent agreement.
- Photo/media release.
- Clear statement of what is included and excluded.

## Minimum Validation Before Running Sessions

- Site walkthrough.
- Emergency plan.
- Check-in/check-out process.
- Restroom access.
- First aid supplies.
- Incident report template.
- Allergies/medical needs process.
- Adult supervision ratios.
- Volunteer boundaries.
- Teen role boundaries.
- Food/snack policy.
- Tool/material safety plan.

## Component-Specific Gates

### Sprout Space

Do not offer childcare until licensing path, staff requirements, insurance, and facility requirements are validated.

### Harvest Cafe

Do not operate as public cafe, caterer, or food-service business until regulatory path is validated.

### Commons Rentals

Do not offer public rentals until facility use, insurance, occupancy, agreements, and supervision/security are validated.

### The Wilds

Do not install or advertise playground/adaptive equipment until site, insurance, safety, accessibility, supervision, and maintenance requirements are validated.

### Paid Teen Roles

Do not pay teens or describe formal employment pathways until federal/state child labor, insurance, payroll, and role restrictions are validated.
