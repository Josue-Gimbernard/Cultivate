# Operations Overview

## Role

Operations owns internal process, execution cadence, admin workflow, finance-sensitive process, and repeatable company systems.

## Current State

Operations has substantial source-derived implications but no approved launch model yet.

## Major Operating Areas

- Childcare licensing and staffing for The Sprout Space.
- Teen worker training/supervision across Sprout Space and Harvest Cafe.
- Cafe food-service compliance and operations.
- Commons rental booking, guidelines, insurance, and after-hours access.
- Grove memberships, day passes, office pods, WiFi, and amenities.
- The Wilds outdoor supervision, safety, accessibility, and maintenance.
- Garden Guild schedule and Growing Grounds care.
- 5-week term calendar.
- Family contribution discounts and Community Fund administration.
