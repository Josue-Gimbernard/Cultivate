# Facility Requirements

## Purpose

Define what a potential Cultivate site must support before serious site selection.

## Facility Principle

The full ecosystem requires a mixed-use family campus. The first viable version may not require every full-campus feature on day one, but the site should not block likely future expansion.

## Required Zones

### Secure Entry And Village Wall

- Clear check-in/check-out path.
- Place for Village Wall near entrance.
- Visitor management.
- Parent/family waiting area.
- Ability to separate public visitors from child program areas.

### The Summit

- Teen lounge/community area.
- Workshop/project tables.
- Storage for tools, arts, media, and projects.
- Kitchen or food-prep adjacency for communal meals.
- Quiet/decompression area.
- Visibility/supervision without feeling institutional.

### The Stepping Stones

- Flexible project room.
- Storage for art, building, science, and kitchen supplies.
- Access to outdoor time.
- Age-appropriate restroom access.
- Ability to separate from teens when needed while allowing bridge programming.

### The Sprout Space

Do not commit to this until licensing path is validated.

Likely needs:

- Secure childcare room(s).
- Age-separated areas for 0-3 and 3-5 if required.
- Child-sized fixtures or licensing-compliant equivalents.
- Nap/rest area.
- Diapering/handwashing.
- Health and incident record storage.
- Outdoor access or compliant play plan.

### The Grove

- Parent work area.
- Reliable high-speed internet.
- Quiet call pods or rooms.
- Desk and lounge mix.
- Printing/basic office amenities.
- Adjacency to cafe if possible.

### The Harvest Cafe

Do not commit to full cafe until food-service path is validated.

Likely needs:

- Service counter.
- Food-prep/storage area.
- Handwashing and dishwashing setup.
- Refrigeration.
- POS/payment setup.
- Menu-specific equipment.
- Seating or shared cafe area.
- Path for Commons catering.

### The Commons

- Flexible room for classes, co-ops, workshops, events, and rentals.
- Tables/chairs storage.
- Display/projector setup.
- After-hours access strategy if rented outside normal hours.
- Restroom access.
- Cleanup and lockup workflow.

### The Wilds

- Secure outdoor boundary.
- Sprout Yard.
- Range.
- Canopy.
- Growing Grounds.
- Shaded areas.
- Accessible routes and surfaces.
- Water access for garden.
- Storage for outdoor tools and supplies.

## Site Questions

- What is the minimum indoor square footage by phase?
- What outdoor area is required for The Wilds?
- Can the site support childcare licensing?
- Can the site support cafe/food service licensing?
- Can zoning support co-working, rentals, events, childcare, food service, and educational enrichment together?
- Is parking/drop-off workable?
- Can public rental traffic be managed without compromising child safety?
