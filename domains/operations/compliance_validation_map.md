# Compliance Validation Map

## Purpose

This file tracks official compliance areas to validate before Cultivate makes public, operational, staffing, facility, childcare, cafe, or launch commitments.

This is not legal advice. Use it as a planning map and verify details with the relevant agency, county, attorney, accountant, insurer, and facility professionals.

## Childcare: The Sprout Space

Likely authority path: Florida Department of Children and Families child-care licensing, unless a local licensing agency applies in the selected county.

Official references:

- Florida DCF Child Care Licensure: https://prod.myflfamilies.com/services/licensing/child-care-licensure
- DCF About Child Care Licensure: https://www.myflfamilies.com/services/child-family/child-care/about-child-care-licensure
- DCF Child Care Laws and Requirements: https://www.myflfamilies.com/services/child-family/child-care/child-care-laws-and-requirements
- DCF Forms and Applications: https://www.myflfamilies.com/services/child-family/child-care/child-care-providers-and-staff/forms-and-applications
- Florida Senate 402.305 licensing standards: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/402.305

Validation questions:

- Is The Sprout Space a child care facility, school-age child care facility, family day care home, large family child care home, or another category?
- Which county will regulate licensing?
- What staff credentials, director credentials, ratios, training, facility requirements, inspections, records, and fire/sanitation approvals apply?
- Can teens work in any capacity in a licensed childcare setting, and under what supervision/training limits?
- What forms, background checks, health records, incident records, and parent agreements are required?

## Food Service: The Harvest Cafe

Likely authority path depends on the final operating model. Florida food operations can fall under DBPR, DOH, or FDACS depending on what is prepared/sold and where.

Official references:

- Florida DOH Food Safety and Sanitation: https://www.floridahealth.gov/environmental-health/food-safety-and-sanitation/index.html
- Florida DOH Hygiene Codes and Standards: https://www.floridahealth.gov/licensing-regulations/regulated-facilities/food-safety-and-sanitation/hygiene-codes-and-standards/
- DBPR Fixed Public Food Service Establishment Application: https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/hr/forms/documents/application_packet_for_fixed_food_service_license.pdf
- Florida Senate 509.241 public food service licenses: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/509.241

Validation questions:

- Is The Harvest Cafe a DBPR public food service establishment, DOH-regulated institutional/limited food service operation, FDACS-regulated food operation, or something else?
- Does the menu include time/temperature control foods?
- Is food prepared onsite, prepackaged, catered, donated, or produced by members?
- Does Commons catering change the license path?
- What plan review, equipment, handwashing, dishwashing, storage, waste, seating, fire, wastewater, and inspection requirements apply?
- What food manager/handler training is required?

## Teen Work Roles

Teen roles appear in the source docs for The Harvest Cafe, The Sprout Space, Garden Guild, and mentorship.

Official references:

- U.S. Department of Labor Child Labor: https://www.dol.gov/whd/childlabor.htm
- U.S. Department of Labor Youth Employment: https://beta.dol.gov/policy-regulations/wage-hour-regulations/pay-benefits/youth-employment
- Florida DOE Child Labor Laws and Information: https://www.fldoe.org/academics/career-adult-edu/career-tech-edu/additional-cte-programs-courses/ojt/childlabor.stml

Validation questions:

- Which teen roles are paid employment versus volunteer/learning roles?
- What age limits, hours, breaks, hazardous occupation restrictions, supervision, and recordkeeping apply?
- Are food service tasks, childcare support tasks, tool use, kitchen equipment, cleaning chemicals, and outdoor maintenance allowed for each age group?
- What proof of age and employment records are required?

## Accessibility And Play Areas: The Wilds

Official reference:

- DOJ 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design: https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/design-standards/2010-stds/

Validation questions:

- Which indoor and outdoor areas are public accommodations or commercial facilities?
- What accessible routes, entrances, restrooms, parking, seating, counters, play-area, and outdoor surface requirements apply?
- What playground safety standards, fall surfaces, fencing, shade, supervision, and adaptive equipment standards apply?
- What local building and zoning approvals are needed?

## Facility, Zoning, Fire, Insurance

Validation questions:

- What zoning category allows the intended mix of childcare, education/enrichment, co-working, cafe, events, rentals, and outdoor play?
- What local fire marshal requirements apply?
- What occupancy classifications apply to each space?
- What parking/drop-off requirements apply?
- What insurance policies are required for childcare, cafe, events, volunteers, minors, outdoor play, and professional services?
- Are therapists/specialists renting Commons required to carry their own insurance and licenses?
