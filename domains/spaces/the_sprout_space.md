# The Sprout Space

## Purpose

On-site childcare for ages 0-5 so families can participate in Cultivate without arranging separate childcare.

## Key Details

- Age groups: 0-3 and 3-5.
- Staffed by qualified lead childcare educators.
- Includes trained, paid teens from The Summit/Glade under direct supervision.
- Open to Cultivate families as an add-on; broader community access may be space-available.
- Designed as warm, calm, play-based, and sensory-rich.

## Requirements To Validate

- Florida DCF licensing.
- Background checks.
- Staff-to-child ratios.
- Waivers and health forms.
- Incident reporting.
- Teen worker training: CPR/first aid, safe sleep, infant safety, mandated reporter, supervised onboarding.
