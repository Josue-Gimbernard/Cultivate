# Spaces Overview

## Role

The Spaces domain owns Cultivate's named physical and experiential components: indoor rooms, outdoor areas, and the relationships among them.

## Current Architecture

Cultivate is designed as a connected ecosystem. The age journey moves from The Sprout Space to The Stepping Stones to The Summit, while parents use The Grove, the broader community enters through The Commons, and The Harvest Cafe connects daily campus life. The Wilds extends the campus outside with play, restoration, and garden learning.

## Current Risks

- Legacy The Glade materials need archival/renaming decisions now that The Summit is current public naming.
- Launch scope could become too large if all spaces are treated as day-one requirements.
- Compliance-heavy spaces need early validation: Sprout Space, Harvest Cafe, The Wilds, and Commons events.
