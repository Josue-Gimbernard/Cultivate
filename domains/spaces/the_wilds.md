# The Wilds

## Purpose

Outdoor campus where Cultivate learning, play, restoration, and garden work move outside.

## Areas

- The Sprout Yard: sensory-friendly outdoor play for ages 0-5.
- The Range: all-ages play and movement space with inclusive/adaptive equipment.
- The Canopy: teen outdoor retreat with hammocks, shade, and unstructured calm.
- The Growing Grounds: working garden supplying learning, meals, and eventually The Harvest Cafe.

## Garden Guild

The Garden Guild is a multi-age group of kids and teens who commit for a 5-week term to daily care of The Growing Grounds. Two to three Guild members tend the garden each day. Contributions are tracked and celebrated on The Village Wall.

## Requirements To Validate

- Safety and supervision model.
- Accessibility and adaptive equipment requirements.
- Outdoor liability/insurance.
- Garden setup, irrigation, soil, and Central Florida seasonal planting plan.
