# The Grove

## Purpose

Parent co-working lounge that lets parents work, connect, and remain near the Cultivate community.

## Key Details

- Desk space, high-speed WiFi, private office pods, lounge seating, printing, office amenities, cafe access.
- Membership options: monthly membership, day pass, family bundle discount.
- Strategic role: removes the awkward waiting barrier for homeschool enrichment and deepens parent involvement.
