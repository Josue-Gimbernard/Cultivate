# The Harvest Cafe

## Purpose

Community cafe and campus gathering point, operated as a real teen business learning experience.

## Key Details

- Serves coffee, tea, smoothies, pastries, healthy snacks, and light bites.
- Teens learn menu planning, food costing, customer service, POS operations, food safety, transactions, marketing, and small-business thinking.
- Supports Commons catering.
- May use food from The Growing Grounds.
- Possible coffee partner: Foxtail or another local brand.

## Requirements To Validate

- Food service licensing and inspections.
- Teen employment and food safety rules.
- Kitchen requirements.
- POS/payment system.
- Cafe partner constraints.
