# The Commons

## Purpose

Multi-use community space for co-ops, classes, workshops, meetings, private events, and rental revenue.

## Key Details

- Serves Cultivate families and broader homeschool/local community.
- Rental types: half-day, full-day, recurring weekly/monthly, after-hours evening/weekend.
- Possible uses: homeschool co-ops, specialist workshops, therapy groups, meetings, celebrations.
- Member discounts, standard community rates, and possible reduced rates for co-ops/nonprofits.
- Optional Harvest Cafe catering.

## Gaps

- Guest capacity is unspecified.
- Rental pricing is unspecified.
- Community use guidelines need drafting.
