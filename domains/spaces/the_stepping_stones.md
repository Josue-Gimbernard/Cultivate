# The Stepping Stones

## Purpose

Community space for ages 6-12, bridging early childhood and the teen program.

## Key Details

- Focuses on hands-on making, discovery, creative exploration, early life skills, and identity/community.
- Includes building, cooking, art, creative media, science, nature, gardening, money basics, communication, and conflict resolution.
- Bridge programming connects older kids with The Summit/Glade through shared meals, joint projects, and mentorship.
- Led by facilitators with education, child development, or youth programming experience.
